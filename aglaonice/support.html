Support

Help with readings, subscription access and app settings.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Get help

For questions, technical problems or feedback about Aglaonice, email contact@dmammedov.dev. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

Aglaonice provides AI-generated lunar and tarot interpretations for entertainment. Readings are not professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

Readings and device compatibility

Lunar Reading is available without a paid subscription. Daily Tarot and Monthly Tarot are subscription features. Some AI-generated readings depend on the availability of Apple Intelligence and the on-device Apple Foundation Models on your device.

If a reading cannot be generated, check whether your device supports Apple Intelligence, whether it is enabled, and whether its models are ready. See Apple's Apple Intelligence information.

Subscriptions, billing and restoration

  • Manage or cancel: Open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then Subscriptions, and select Aglaonice. You can also follow Apple's cancellation instructions.
  • Restore access: In Aglaonice, open the subscription screen and use Restore Purchases if shown. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Refund requests: Apple handles App Store payments and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting Aglaonice does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

Voice and notifications

Speech quality and voice availability depend on the voices installed on your device. You can review voice options and related settings within iOS. If spoken readings are silent, check your audio output, sound settings and downloaded voices.

To change reminders, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Aglaonice and adjust notification permissions.
